About Us

A cinematic eye for the everyday

Frames Of Motion (FOM) Studios is a creative media brand specialising in photography, videography and visual storytelling. We focus on capturing moments with precision, emotion and a cinematic edge.

Our work spans sports, automotives, events and much more. We aim to deliver high-quality visuals that tell powerful stories and elevate brands.
