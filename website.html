Website

Change your contact details, socials, hours and announcement banner here. Press Publish and fomstudios.co.za updates within seconds, with no GitHub upload.

Announcement banner

A strip across the very top of every page, for things like "December bookings open".

Contact details

Updates the phone, email and WhatsApp buttons and links on every page, including the booking form.

Social links

Instagram replaces the existing links. The others appear in the footer once filled in.

Location and hours

Shown in the footer on every page and on the contact page.

Blocked dates

Holidays, days closed, or dates you are already booked. On the website, the booking form refuses these dates and shows your label. Leave "To" empty for a single day.

Anything you leave blank keeps the details already written on the site, so the website never shows an empty gap.
